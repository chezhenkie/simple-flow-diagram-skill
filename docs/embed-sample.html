What happens after the pay button

Engineering notes, 24 November 2026

A card payment looks instant at the checkout. Behind the button, the request passes a fraud check, reaches the issuer, and comes back approved or declined.

The diagram below follows one request. It is a single HTML page, embedded here unchanged, with its own controls.

A decline returns along the same path with a code, so the checkout can show the reason and offer another card.
